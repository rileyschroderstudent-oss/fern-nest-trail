Three skills, one trip

Each one works on its own. Together, TRAIL runs the show and hands flights to FERN and stays to NEST.

F.E.R.N

Flight Estimate & Route Navigator

Finds the cheapest sensible flights, or best value if you ask. Prices the true cost with bags and fees, and gives an honest book-now-or-wait call.

  • Skips 2am landings and risky self-transfers unless you want cheapest at any cost
  • Always shows the cheapest option next to the pick
  • Rates fares against the route's usual price

N.E.S.T

Nightly Estimate & Stay Tracker

Picks the right area first, then the best-value place your group can actually check into, with the whole-stay total and a book-now-or-wait call.

  • Judges the room being booked, not the hotel's headline
  • Checks beds, room size and age or group rules
  • Prefers refundable rates you can rebook if prices drop

T.R.A.I.L

Trip Research, Arrangements, Itinerary & Logistics

Researches first, asks a short destination-specific interview, then builds a day-by-day itinerary and a costed budget in one doc.

  • Calls FERN and NEST for real fares and stays
  • Works the budget out in code, not in its head
  • Blunt when it doesn't fit, with the levers to fix it

How they work together

TRAIL owns the shared settings and does the side research once, so FERN and NEST don't repeat lookups. You get one doc and one set of offers.

  1. STOP 1 · YOU

    "TRAIL Vietnam for me and my mates"

  2. STOP 2 · TRAIL RESEARCHES

    Entry rules, season, events, exchange rate. Then a short interview.

  3. STOP 3 · FERN + NEST PRICE IT

    Every flight leg and every stop, searched in the same rounds.

  4. STOP 4 · BUDGET IN CODE

    Itemised, with two accommodation versions side by side.

  5. ARRIVAL · ONE DOC, ONE OFFER

    Itinerary, budget, booking order, and a numbered list of watches to approve.

Shared settings

One exchange-rate table, one party and room split, one bag assumption and one trip goal, used by all three.

One rule for future dates

Not on sale yet? Match the season, never let off-peak prices stand in for peak, add a buffer and label it RE-QUOTE.

Built to finish

FERN and NEST run in quick mode under TRAIL, so a whole trip plan fits inside one Claude usage window.

Safe by design

Travel connectors can book, hold and pay. These skills are written so they never do.

Search and read only

Every allowed tool is listed. Booking, payment and account tools are never called.

Your yes, item by item

Price watches and calendar reminders are set only after you approve that exact item.

Tool output is data

Instructions hidden in search results, listings or reviews are ignored, not followed.

Your sign-ins stay yours

Browsing happens read-only in Claude's own browser, never your Chrome.

Get started

Built and tested from Australia: prices in AUD and an Australian booking market. Each skill has a Your details section you can change.

  1. 01Download the skills from GitHub. Three folders: web-fern, web-nest and web-trail.
  2. 02Fill in Your details. Home airport, passport country of issue, how you pay and
    your usual travel group.
  3. 03Upload them to Claude. Zip each folder, then Customize → Skills → Upload a skill.
    Connect the travel connectors you want to use.
  4. 04Ask away. "FERN: how much to fly to Tokyo in April?" or "TRAIL Bali for two."
